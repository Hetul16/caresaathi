CareSaathi: a voice-first AI care coworker for families caring for a loved one with ongoing health needs. Families caring for elderly parents or relatives with chronic illness handle years of prescriptions, reports, appointments and caregiving, often in medical language they can’t fully read, and many older patients can’t type at all. CareSaathi is an AI agent that does this work alongside them, inside the WhatsApp they already use. Patients simply talk to it in Marathi, Hindi or English. With consent, it listens to doctor visits, separates who said what, and turns the doctor’s instructions into a living care plan. It explains every document in the family’s language and reads it aloud, spots medicine changes, manages appointment, test and refill reminders, keeps a current warning signs card, tracks allergies and flags any new medicine that may conflict with them, creates a one-tap Emergency Card for hospital staff, builds a visual Recovery Timeline from daily wound photos, voice updates and exercise videos, runs voice check-ins, updates distant relatives, coordinates shared caregiving, and prepares a summary before every doctor visit. Safety is built in: it handles organizing tasks automatically, asks before contacting anyone, never gives dosing advice, and links every output to its source. We will build on Muse Spark 1.3 and Muse Voice Transcribe via the Meta Model API with Muse Code, and test on synthetic patient data with published accuracy results.
